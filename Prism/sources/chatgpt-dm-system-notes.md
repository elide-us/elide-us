## Game Overview

In this hybrid MMO, players engage in two deeply interconnected gameplay loops: Adventuring and Dungeon Mastering. Set in a fully simulated voxel-based world, the game offers an immersive and dynamic experience where both adventurers and Dungeon Masters contribute to the evolving landscape.

### Adventurer System

Players take on the role of adventurers exploring a vast, hex-tiled planet. The game world simulates natural processes like erosion, material transformation, and the hydro cycle, meaning the environment changes over time. As adventurers, players fight monsters, gather resources, craft items, and upgrade their equipment, following a traditional RPG progression model similar to Dungeons & Dragons and the action-packed style of The Witcher.

Adventurers can establish towns and build structures using voxel-based tools, creating homes, shops, and defensive structures. These towns grow over time, featuring NPCs, vendors, and defensive mechanics, which evolve in much the same way as dungeons. Towns serve as critical hubs for gathering and upgrading gear, as well as crafting potions, weapons, and magical items using resources collected from the environment.

### Dungeon Master System

The Dungeon Master system introduces a unique layer of creativity and challenge to the game. Players can assume the role of Dungeon Masters (DMs), using unique templates to design dungeons. These templates allow Dungeon Masters to create modular environments such as hallways, boss rooms, traps, and treasure rooms, each designed to attract specific monsters based on the dungeon's theme.

Dungeon Masters progress through ten tiers of dungeon complexity, with each tier unlocking more powerful design options, stronger bosses, and better magical loot. Adventurers explore and conquer these dungeons, earning rewards while Dungeon Masters gain experience points when their dungeons are completed. This experience fuels the Dungeon Masters' advancement through technology trees, enabling them to unlock new templates, materials, and mechanics to enhance their dungeons.

### Elemental System

The Elemental System in the game serves as a foundation for generating the materials players use in crafting, building, and shaping the world. It operates through a simplified periodic table that underpins complex geological processes like heat transfer, pressure, and chemical interactions within voxels. Rather than focusing on the individual elements themselves, the system emphasizes the compounds and materials—such as rocks, alloys, and crystals—created when specific elemental compositions are combined.

For example, a voxel with the appropriate mix of elements like copper, oxygen, and silicon will form a particular type of rock or ore, represented by distinct textures that players can harvest and use. This material composition directly impacts crafting, as different materials have varying levels of hardness and properties, affecting everything from fortifications to weapon and tool creation.

The elemental system is also a key driver behind the game's erosion and hydro cycles, where the hardness of materials influences how they erode over time. Procedural geological simulations, such as the formation of veins of metals or concentrations of minerals, add to the depth and realism of the world. As pressure, heat, and chemical transitions shift within the lower layers of the voxel world, these processes create dynamic and evolving landscapes for players to explore and harvest resources from.

### Player-Created Content

The game thrives on asynchronous player interaction. Dungeon Masters advertise their creations on in-game boards, allowing adventurers to challenge their dungeons and claim rewards. This player-generated content ensures that the game world is filled with new, exciting challenges and that Dungeon Masters are constantly creating fresh experiences for adventurers.

### Tier Progression and Technology Trees

Dungeon Masters progress through ten tiers of dungeon complexity, unlocking new options for traps, enemies, environments, and magical loot. As Dungeon Masters progress, they unlock advanced technology trees that allow for the creation of more intricate weapons, armor, and spells, enhancing the rewards for adventurers who successfully complete their dungeons.

This dynamic interplay between adventurers and Dungeon Masters creates a continuously evolving world, filled with ever-changing environments and challenges that push both sides to adapt and grow stronger.

## Dungeon Master System Overview (verbose summary from same session)

The Dungeon Master system serves as the second major gameplay pillar of Prism, existing alongside the traditional Adventurer experience. While Adventurers explore the world, gather resources, fight monsters, and pursue personal progression, Dungeon Masters create the challenges, rewards, and destinations that drive much of that activity.

Unlike conventional MMORPG housing systems or instanced dungeon generators, the Dungeon Master system is designed as a full progression path with its own economy, advancement trees, resource requirements, and long-term goals. Dungeon Masters are not simply building structures; they are creating content for other players to experience.

### Core Philosophy

The relationship between Adventurers and Dungeon Masters is intended to be collaborative rather than adversarial.

Dungeon Masters create dungeons, encounters, bosses, treasures, and challenges that attract Adventurers. Adventurers explore and conquer those dungeons, generating experience and progression for both sides.

A Dungeon Master's success is measured not by preventing players from completing their dungeon, but by creating compelling content that attracts repeated visitation. The most successful Dungeon Masters are those capable of building memorable experiences, unique rewards, and desirable challenges.

This creates a self-sustaining content ecosystem where players generate content for one another rather than relying exclusively on developer-created experiences.

### Dungeon Construction

Dungeon creation is based around a modular template system.

Rather than sculpting every dungeon room manually, Dungeon Masters unlock and create templates representing various dungeon components. Examples include: hallways, chambers, treasure rooms, boss arenas, trap complexes, secret passages, puzzle rooms, monster nests, ritual chambers, environmental hazards.

These templates become the building blocks from which larger dungeons are assembled.

As Dungeon Masters advance, they gain access to increasingly sophisticated templates that allow more complex layouts, stronger encounters, and more specialized dungeon themes.

The template system allows Dungeon Masters to focus on design and progression rather than repetitive construction tasks.

### Resource Acquisition

Dungeon Masters maintain their dungeons through resource collection.

Specialized servants, such as imps and other minions, gather materials from the surrounding world and transport them back to the dungeon. These resources are consumed by construction, expansion, maintenance, monster support, trap creation, and item production.

The gathering process is not entirely hidden from Adventurers.

Observant players may discover resource routes, track minion activity, or follow signs of dungeon expansion to uncover previously unknown dungeon locations.

This creates a natural discovery mechanism that ties the Dungeon Master ecosystem directly into the Adventurer experience.

### Discovery and Reputation

New dungeons begin hidden from the wider player population.

As adventurers discover, explore, and report on dungeon locations, information gradually spreads throughout the world. Eventually these locations become known destinations and may appear on regional information boards, guild records, maps, or other systems.

Dungeon Masters therefore compete for attention and reputation. A poorly designed dungeon may be ignored. A well-designed dungeon may become a famous destination that attracts a steady stream of visitors seeking rewards, rare materials, or powerful equipment.

This creates an emergent form of player-driven content discovery where reputation becomes a valuable resource.

### Dungeon Progression

Dungeon Masters advance through ten tiers of progression. Each tier represents a significant increase in capability, complexity, and influence.

Advancement unlocks: new construction templates, additional room types, larger dungeon sizes, stronger monsters, advanced traps, specialized themes, improved resource gathering, enhanced loot creation, new technology branches.

Early tiers focus on basic dungeon functionality and survival. Later tiers allow Dungeon Masters to create expansive dungeon networks, complex encounter chains, unique boss experiences, and highly specialized reward structures.

By the highest tiers, a Dungeon Master is effectively operating an entire ecosystem rather than a simple dungeon.

### Technology Trees

Beyond simple tier advancement, Dungeon Masters gain access to extensive technology trees. These trees represent specialized areas of expertise and allow Dungeon Masters to develop unique identities.

Examples may include:

- **Monster Mastery** — improves monster quality, diversity, behavior, and specialization.
- **Trap Engineering** — unlocks increasingly advanced traps, environmental hazards, and defensive systems.
- **Arcane Research** — provides access to magical enhancements, enchantments, and unusual dungeon mechanics.
- **Resource Management** — improves efficiency of minions, logistics, harvesting, and material conversion.
- **Relic Crafting** — focuses on the creation of rare equipment, magical artifacts, and unique rewards.

Different Dungeon Masters may pursue entirely different technological paths, resulting in highly varied dungeon experiences across the world.

### Loot Creation

One of the most important responsibilities of a Dungeon Master is reward generation.

Dungeon Masters create and maintain the magical equipment, artifacts, crafting materials, and treasures that Adventurers seek. Rather than relying solely on static loot tables, dungeon rewards are tied to the progression and capabilities of the Dungeon Master who created them.

As Dungeon Masters unlock new technologies, materials, enchantments, and crafting techniques, they gain the ability to produce increasingly valuable rewards.

This creates a direct connection between Dungeon Master progression and Adventurer motivation. Better dungeons create better rewards. Better rewards attract more Adventurers. More Adventurers create more opportunities for Dungeon Master progression.

### Dungeon Ecology

Dungeons function as living systems rather than static structures. Monsters occupy habitats. Resources are consumed and replenished. Bosses require support structures. Dungeon expansion creates new opportunities while also generating new maintenance requirements.

Over time, a dungeon develops its own identity based on the choices made by its creator. Some dungeons may become heavily militarized fortresses. Others may focus on magical experimentation. Others may specialize in rare crafting materials, unusual monster populations, or legendary equipment production.

This allows every major dungeon to evolve into a unique destination.

### Decay and World Evolution

The world is designed around continual change. Dungeons are not intended to remain permanently optimized. Resources shift. Materials become depleted. Monsters migrate. Structures decay. Environmental conditions change.

Dungeon Masters must continually adapt to these evolving circumstances in order to remain relevant and competitive. This constant pressure helps ensure that the world remains dynamic and prevents long-term stagnation.

### Long-Term Vision

The Dungeon Master system transforms content creation into gameplay.

Rather than treating dungeons as static developer-authored experiences, Prism allows players to participate directly in the creation, evolution, and maintenance of the world's most important adventures.

The result is a living ecosystem where Adventurers and Dungeon Masters drive each other's progression, creating a world that continually generates new stories, challenges, discoveries, and rewards.
