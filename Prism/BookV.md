# V. Setting and Anthropy

The Realms of Prism today are seven. But it was not always so.

In the time before, all realms were unified, existing together in harmony. Magic flowed freely, and the elements intertwined, creating a world of balance and unity. But this era of oneness came to a violent end with the rise of the Lonely Muse, whose relentless pursuit of power and truth threatened to unravel the very fabric of the universe.

To stop her and to preserve reality itself, the Prism was shattered. The **Titans**, led by the wisdom of the **First Titan**, made the choice to divide the world, creating a new order to contain the Muse’s power. Each realm was given its place in this new, fragmented reality, organized according to the very design of magic itself.

## Canon Rulings

A running ledger of rulings that resolve ambiguities between source documents. Each entry is dated; where a book conflicts with a ruling, the ruling wins until the book is revised.

- **2026-07-02 — Life is Green.** Life magic belongs to the Green school (Earth and Life): the domain of the god of the vine, magic for green and growing things. White is Light and Prophecy only. Healing therefore splits across the White/Green seam — White protects and wards (Thaumaturgy), Green grows and mends (Biomancy). Any earlier reference to a superhuman "Life/Light" school is deprecated; Life is mortal magic.

- **2026-07-02 — Schools are two-domain.** Every school spans two domains (Red is Fire *and* Blood; Green is Earth *and* Life). Single-element school names are shorthand only. The "four elements" accessible to mortals refer to the four Mortal Realm schools: Green (Earth/Life), Yellow (Air/Lightning), Red (Fire/Blood), Blue (Water/Illusion). The three Ethereal/Light schools — White, Orange, Black — are the superhuman tier.

- **2026-07-02 — The seven stats are canonical.** One stat per school, as tabled above: Strength (Red), Dexterity (Yellow), Constitution (Black), Intelligence (Blue), Wisdom (White), Endurance (Green), Luck (Orange). Stats measure *capacities*, not actions — Charisma is excluded because it is an action, not a stat. Constitution and Endurance do not collapse: Constitution (Black) is the capacity to be hit; Endurance (Green) is the capacity for sustained effort. Combat is Dark Souls–style action combat, and every stat must cash out in combat-relevant terms. Charism checks, if required, would use Luck instead.

- **2026-07-02 — Celestial events are global.** Celestial configurations (eclipses, alignments) modify the whole world at once, across all dungeon tiers and regions — not endgame-only bonuses. Scarcity and abundance are deterministic and foretold in the sky.
