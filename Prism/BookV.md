# V. Setting and Anthropy

The Realms of Prism today are seven. But it was not always so.

In the time before, all realms were unified, existing together in harmony. Magic flowed freely, and the elements intertwined, creating a world of balance and unity. But this era of oneness came to a violent end with the rise of the Lonely Muse, whose relentless pursuit of power and truth threatened to unravel the very fabric of the universe.

To stop her and to preserve reality itself, the Prism was shattered. The **Titans**, led by the wisdom of the **First Titan**, made the choice to divide the world, creating a new order to contain the Muse’s power. Each realm was given its place in this new, fragmented reality, organized according to the very design of magic itself.

**Cosmology ruling (2026-07-02, ledgers 05840ADF, 8543A752).** The realms are distilled into the celestial system: there are **seven instances of the world, each a different body, all sharing the same universe and the same celestial simulation**. The hex planets' pentagon zones are **gateways into the other realms**. The seven sky-planets are refractions of the one home reality (source: the 2026-07-01 celestial session, archived at `sources/celestial-cosmology-session-2026-07-01.md`).

Each world has twelve pentagon points (hex-sphere topology, Book III). These are **Advent locations** (ledgers 73EA6D39, 35A9CEAE): **unreachable by design** — world generation constrains them beyond the survivable line (the working idea, idea-grade: six as mountains, six as deep ocean), because pentagon spaces are not part of the standard world — entering one removes you from it. The zones around them are anomalous — "weird," in the register of the Bermuda Triangle mythos, with all of the Berserk meaning of "Advent" attached — and the realm portals **surround these points**, giving them their reason to exist. What lies through them is hand-crafted: unique zones and alien, magical realms where the rules of the universe shift. A fog wall that teleports any player who reaches one "elsewhere" is an open idea; the specific transit mechanics are undecided.

**The body order** (ruled; inner → outer):

1. **Chaos** — innermost; red, orange, and black
2. **Fire** — red
3. **Home** — the habitable band; carries the moon
4. **Earth** — brown and green
5. **Light** — the white gas giant *(ruled 2026-07-02: the source session's "Life/Light" was human error; White is Light and Prophecy — ledger D30A32BF)*
6. **Air** — the ringed planet; white and pale blue
7. **Water** — Neptune blue
8. **Death** — outermost; black, occulted behind the star, seen only as a shadow transiting the galactic cloud

The sun anchors the system and the moon rides with Home — both outside the seven. All seven planets share one apparent size, about a quarter of the sun/moon disc. **The moon is not a simulated body**: it tracks the real-world Moon one-to-one — true synodic cycle, phase and position from real ephemeris (ledger D2AAFEEC). Time runs at one in-game minute per five real seconds — a two-hour in-game day.

The seven sky-planets map one per school (ledger D30A32BF): Chaos → Orange, Fire → Red, Earth → Green (Earth *and* Life), Light → White, Air → Yellow, Water → Blue, Death → Black.

## Canon Rulings

A running ledger of rulings that resolve ambiguities between source documents. Each entry is dated; where a book conflicts with a ruling, the ruling wins until the book is revised.

- **2026-07-02 — Life is Green.** Life magic belongs to the Green school (Earth and Life): the domain of the god of the vine, magic for green and growing things. White is Light and Prophecy only. Healing therefore splits across the White/Green seam — White protects and wards (Thaumaturgy), Green grows and mends (Biomancy). Any earlier reference to a superhuman "Life/Light" school is deprecated; Life is mortal magic.

- **2026-07-02 — Schools are two-domain.** Every school spans two domains (Red is Fire *and* Blood; Green is Earth *and* Life). Single-element school names are shorthand only. The "four elements" accessible to mortals refer to the four Mortal Realm schools: Green (Earth/Life), Yellow (Air/Lightning), Red (Fire/Blood), Blue (Water/Illusion). The three Ethereal/Light schools — White, Orange, Black — are the superhuman tier.

- **2026-07-02 — The seven stats are canonical.** One stat per school, as tabled above: Strength (Red), Dexterity (Yellow), Constitution (Black), Intelligence (Blue), Wisdom (White), Endurance (Green), Luck (Orange). Stats measure *capacities*, not actions — Charisma is excluded because it is an action, not a stat. Constitution and Endurance do not collapse: Constitution (Black) is the capacity to be hit; Endurance (Green) is the capacity for sustained effort. Combat is Dark Souls–style action combat, and every stat must cash out in combat-relevant terms. Charisma checks, if required, would use Luck instead.

- **2026-07-02 — Celestial events are global.** Celestial configurations (eclipses, alignments) modify the whole world at once, across all dungeon tiers and regions — not endgame-only bonuses. Scarcity and abundance are deterministic and foretold in the sky.
